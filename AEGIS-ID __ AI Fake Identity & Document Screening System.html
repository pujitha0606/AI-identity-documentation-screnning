High-Frequency Compression Delta Analysis

Error Level Analysis (ELA) works by intentionally re-saving an image at a known quality level (e.g., 90%) and computing the difference matrix between the original and resaved frames. In an authentic document, the error levels are uniformly dispersed. In edited or spliced documents (such as replaced portrait photos or altered expiration digits), the spliced regions exhibit sharp, unnatural thermal luminance spikes.

Forensic Color Key:
• Dark Blue / Indigo: Uniform baseline compression (Authentic)
• Bright Red / White Hotspots: Re-saved spliced artifacts (Tampering)
